EmpiresDex — Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 5, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how EmpiresDex ("the Bot") may collect, use, and store information when users interact with it on Discord.

2. Information That May Be Processed

Depending on the features you use, EmpiresDex may process:

- Discord user IDs.
- Discord server IDs.
- Usernames and display names.
- Collectible ownership and collection records.
- Trading history and game statistics.
- Bot command interactions and configuration data.

The Bot does not request your Discord password or authentication credentials.

3. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

- Operate the Bot and its features.
- Maintain player profiles and collections.
- Process trades and collectible ownership.
- Prevent abuse and investigate technical problems.
- Maintain and improve the service.

4. Data Storage

Information may be stored in the Bot's database and infrastructure for as long as necessary to operate the service.

Access to stored information should be limited to authorized administrators.

5. Data Sharing

EmpiresDex does not intentionally sell users' personal information.

Information may be processed by Discord and infrastructure providers required to operate the Bot. Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Some information may remain stored while an account or game feature is active.

For questions about accessing or requesting deletion of information, contact the EmpiresDex owner through the official support server or the contact method provided by the owner.

Certain information may need to be retained for technical, security, or operational reasons.

7. Security

Reasonable measures should be taken to protect stored information. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.
